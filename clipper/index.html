Clipper is an internal tool by Gaming Is Dead Studio. It is not offered to the public.

About Clipper

It uploads our own edited highlight videos to YouTube channels we own and manage, sets their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reads those channels’ statistics. It does not access any other YouTube user’s data.

Clipper uses YouTube API Services.

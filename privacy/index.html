Website delivery and hosting

This website is delivered through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit the website, technical connection data such as your IP address, the requested page, date and time, browser information, and referrer may be processed to deliver and secure the website.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the reliable and secure provision of this website. Technical data may be processed by GitHub in the United States and other countries in accordance with its applicable safeguards. Further information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies and local game data

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or marketing cookies.

The games use your browser's local storage to keep game progress and settings such as language, volume, display, and cursor preferences. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the website operator. It is used only to provide the game functionality you request. You can remove it through your browser's site-data settings or the reset controls offered by a game.

Because no non-essential tracking storage is used, the website does not display a consent banner.

Steam Signal images and external links

Steam Signal displays game images from Steam's content-delivery network. Loading those images sends technical connection data, including your IP address, to the Steam content provider. Image requests are made without the page URL as a referrer. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is presenting the listed games in a useful and recognizable form. See the Steam Privacy Policy.

Links to GitHub, Steam, Discord, Google Drive, AdSimple, and other external websites transfer you to the respective provider only when you choose to follow them. Their own privacy terms then apply.

Contact

If you contact the operator by email, the information you provide is processed to respond to your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contract-related enquiries and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other enquiries. Correspondence is retained only as long as necessary for the request and any applicable legal obligations.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority.
